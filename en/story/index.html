Chapter 1

The crisis that changed everything

During the Corona crisis, over 48,000 hospitality businesses in Germany closed their doors forever. One of them belonged to our co-founder's family. A restaurant that had existed for over 20 years. Destroyed in just a few months.

"We saw how hard-working people lost everything – not because their food was bad, but because they couldn't manage the digital transformation in time."

Chapter 2

We understand your daily reality

We know the stress behind the counter. The 14-hour days. The aching feet. The bills piling up. When are you supposed to find time for "digitalization"?

And then the big platforms come and take up to 30% from every order. At the end of the month, barely anything is left – and the customers? They don't even belong to you.

  • No time for technology
  • Up to 30% commission to platforms
  • Third-party branding instead of your own
  • Customer data belongs to others
  • Dependent on algorithms
Chapter 3

Good solutions, but at what price?

Yes, they exist – the big platforms. Lieferando, Wolt, Uber Eats. They've driven digitalization forward and offer functioning systems. We acknowledge that.

But the price? 13% to 30% from every single order. With 10,000€ monthly revenue, that's up to 3,000€ – every month. Money that should actually belong to you.

"Many restaurateurs tell us: We know the commissions are too high. But what should we do? We don't see an alternative." – That's exactly what we want to change.
