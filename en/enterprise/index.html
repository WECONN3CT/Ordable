Frequently Asked Questions

Questions?

Find answers to the most common questions about ORDABLE.

How fast can I be online with ORDABLE?

Typically, you'll be online with your complete solution within 7-14 days. We take care of everything: website creation, app development, and order terminal setup. You don't have to do anything yourself.

Are there hidden costs or commissions?

No! With ORDABLE, you only pay your monthly fixed price. There are no commissions on orders, no setup fees, and no hidden costs. What you see is what you pay.

Can I transfer my existing menu?

Absolutely! We'll transfer your complete menu including all extras, variants, and allergens. Just send us your current menu and we'll take care of the rest.

Which payment methods are supported?

We support all common payment methods: PayPal, credit card, Klarna, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and of course cash on delivery. Transaction fees are minimal and transparent.

How does support work?

Our support team is here for you. With the Professional plan, you get 24/7 support via phone, email, and WhatsApp. Average response time: under 15 minutes. We speak your language and understand hospitality.
